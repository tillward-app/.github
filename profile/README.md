# Tillward

Rules your checkout keeps.

Tillward is a small independent team in Hanoi, Vietnam. We make three Shopify apps for checkout rules.

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Tillward Order Limits](https://apps.shopify.com/nexo-order-limit) | Cap quantity, order value and weight per order, and what one customer, a customer group or the whole store can buy over a day, a month or a lifetime. Each limit shows on your storefront and runs again inside Shopify checkout. |
| [Tillward Payment Rules](https://apps.shopify.com/nexo-checkout-customizer) | Hide, move or rename the payment methods shown at Shopify checkout by country, cart total, tags, B2B buyer or PO box address. Rules run inside checkout. |
| [Tillward Age Verification](https://apps.shopify.com/nexo-age-verification) | An age gate on your storefront: a yes or no click, a birth year, a date of birth or a checkbox. The buyer declares their age; the app does not check ID. |
